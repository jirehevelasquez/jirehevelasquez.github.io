About Me

I am a fourth-year mechanical engineering student with experience across mechanical design, aerospace systems, mechatronics, robotics, manufacturing, testing, and hands-on technical work. My projects emphasize the full engineering process: defining the problem, designing and analyzing a solution, building prototypes, testing hardware, debugging systems, and documenting the results.

This portfolio highlights projects involving embedded systems, UAV localization, compressor rotor stiffness analysis, mechanical restoration, composites, robotics, aircraft work, and other design-build-test experiences.
