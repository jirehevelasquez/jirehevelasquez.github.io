About This Portfolio

This portfolio highlights my engineering work through project-based examples of design, analysis, prototyping, manufacturing, testing, troubleshooting, and technical documentation. My background combines aerospace engineering experience, mechatronics coursework, embedded systems, hands-on aircraft and automotive work, composite fabrication, CNC/test specimen manufacturing, and formal design-review processes.

I use this site as a central place to document projects beyond a traditional resume. Each project page is structured around the engineering problem, my role, the tools and processes used, design decisions, testing or analysis performed, results, lessons learned, and supporting media such as photos, CAD images, diagrams, test data, GitHub repositories, or technical documentation.
